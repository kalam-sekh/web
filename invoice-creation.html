A Look Inside Invoice Creation

Real screens from the workspace — quotations, invoices, and print-ready bills in one place.

Your Billing Workspace, One Screen Away

Start from a clear home screen with separate Quotation and Invoice cards, then jump straight into Create Quotation, Create Invoice, Print, or Manage Records from the quick actions. No hunting through menus to get a bill out the door.

Quotations Invoices Print Manage Records

Create an Invoice in Minutes

Enter the customer's name, address, and date, choose Normal Bill (no GST) or GST Bill (18% GST), then add items with description, quantity, unit, and unit price. Gross, discount, GST, and net total update as you go.

Customer Details GST 18% Multiple Items Auto Totals

Clean Quotations, Ready to Send

Prepare estimates with the same simple form. Add customer details, pick the bill type, list items with a discount if needed, and save and print the quotation in a single step.

Print-Ready Business Documents

Preview any quotation or invoice before it goes out. Choose the document type, toggle the company logo, tax details, and totals, then print or save as PDF. Every document ends with an authorized signature line and a thank-you note.
