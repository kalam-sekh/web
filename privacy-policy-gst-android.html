1. Information we collect

The App may collect or process the following information when you use its features:

  • Account information: your email address and authentication information required to create and access your account. Passwords are handled through the App's authentication system and are not displayed to AKI Tech.
  • Business information: business name, GSTIN, business address, contact details, logo, and other information you choose to add to your business profile or invoices.
  • Customer and invoice information: customer names, GSTINs, addresses, contact details, product or service descriptions, quantities, prices, taxes, discounts, payment status, quotations, and other invoice-related records you enter.
  • Support communications: information you provide when you contact AKI Tech for assistance.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic, security, and app-performance information may be processed when necessary to operate, protect, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

Please do not enter information into the App that you are not authorized to use or process.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • provide invoice, quotation, customer, business-record, PDF, export, and sharing features;
  • store and display records associated with your account;
  • provide support and respond to requests;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, and diagnose technical problems;
  • improve the reliability and functionality of the App; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing of information

AKI Tech does not sell your personal information.

Information may be disclosed only in the following circumstances:

  • At your direction: when you choose to export, download, print, or share an invoice, quotation, PDF, or other record.
  • Service providers: to providers that support essential functions such as authentication, hosting, storage, security, diagnostics, or technical operations. They may process information only to provide services to us and subject to appropriate obligations.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, enforce our rights, investigate fraud or abuse, or protect users and the public.
  • Business changes: as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of the App, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

4. Storage and retention

We retain account and App data for as long as needed to provide the App, maintain your account, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. When an account-deletion request is completed, the account and associated personal data are deleted or anonymized, except for information that must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory reason. Any retained information is kept only for the required period.

Files that you export or share outside the App are controlled by you and by the receiving application or service. Deleting your GST Invoice Maker account does not automatically remove copies previously exported, downloaded, printed, or shared by you.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential and for securing exported invoice files.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your account. You can update editable information in the App or contact us using the details below. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before completing it.

7. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your GST Invoice Maker account and its associated data in either of these ways:

  • Inside the App: open the account or settings area and select the account-deletion option.
  • Outside the App: email AKI Tech from your registered email address using the button below. Use the subject GST Invoice Maker Account Deletion Request and identify the account you want deleted.
Request account deletion

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account. After verification, we will process the request and delete or anonymize associated account data, subject to any limited retention required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory obligations. Account deactivation alone is not used as a substitute for deletion.

8. Children's privacy

GST Invoice Maker is a business productivity application and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so that we can review and address the matter.

9. Third-party services and links

The App may interact with third-party applications or services when you choose to share or export content. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies. AKI Tech is not responsible for the privacy practices of services that we do not control.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, legal requirements, or our data practices. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or another appropriate method.
