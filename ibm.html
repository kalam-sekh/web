A Look Inside IBM

Real screens from the platform — one workspace for every part of your business.

All-in-One Business Workspace

Every module lives on one home screen — Employees, Attendance, Payroll, Inventory, Sales & Orders, Ecommerce, Purchase, CRM, Expenses, Accounts, Assets, Contacts, Reports, Help Desk and File Manager — so your team never has to switch apps.

Smart Inventory Control

A live view of total products, in-stock and low-stock counts, and warehouses, paired with a product inventory table and a recent transactions feed — so stock levels are never a guess.

Sales & Orders Made Easy

Quotations, sales orders, invoices, deliveries, and payments flow through one clean pipeline — track every customer from "sent" to "paid" without leaving the screen.

Reports That Help You Grow

Sales and expense trends, account balances, and profit charts come together on one dashboard, with one-click export to Excel, PDF, or a summary report for faster decisions.
