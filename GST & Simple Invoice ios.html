Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

AK International ("we", "our", or "us") operates the GST & Simple Invoice mobile application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use GST & Simple Invoice. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly.

1. Information You Provide

While using GST & Simple Invoice, you may enter information such as:

  • Business name and business details
  • GST or tax information
  • Customer names and contact information
  • Product and service information
  • Invoice, quotation, and billing information
  • Payment and transaction details entered by you
  • Business address, phone number, or email address

This information is provided by you for the purpose of creating and managing invoices, quotations, estimates, customers, products, services, and other business records within the App.

2. How We Use Information

Information entered into the App may be used to provide features such as:

  • Creating GST and non-GST invoices
  • Generating quotations and estimates
  • Calculating taxes, totals, and invoice amounts
  • Managing customers, products, and services
  • Saving and retrieving invoice records
  • Generating and sharing invoice documents
  • Improving the usability and functionality of the App

3. Storage of Information

Information you enter into GST & Simple Invoice may be stored locally on your device or through storage services used by the App, depending on the features available in your version of the App.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and any business or customer information stored through the App.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared only when:

  • You choose to share an invoice, quotation, estimate, or other document.
  • It is required to provide a feature or service requested by you.
  • We are required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate permission or lawful basis to collect, store, and use your customers' personal information.

6. Invoice and Financial Information

The App may process billing, invoice, GST, tax, and transaction information entered by you.

GST & Simple Invoice is an invoicing and record-management tool. Unless specifically stated otherwise, it does not act as a bank, payment processor, accountant, tax authority, or financial institution.

Users are responsible for verifying invoice amounts, GST calculations, tax rates, financial records, and compliance with applicable tax and accounting requirements.
